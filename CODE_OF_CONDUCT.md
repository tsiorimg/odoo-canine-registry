# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Open Canine Registry to be a welcoming, professional, and constructive open-source project.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus feedback on the work;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- document technical and functional decisions;
- protect personal, confidential, and production data;
- respect copyright, licenses, and trademarks;
- disclose conflicts of interest that may affect a contribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, unauthorized use of third-party data, and attempts to compromise project infrastructure are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or security concerns privately to the project maintainer. The maintainer may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related community spaces.
