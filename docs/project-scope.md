# Project scope

## Purpose

Open Canine Registry is an open-source registry-management platform built for Odoo 19 Community. It aims to support structured canine identity, breeding, registration, validation, document, payment, and public-search workflows.

## MVP outcome

The MVP must demonstrate an end-to-end workflow:

1. a portal user manages a canine owner profile;
2. the owner records a dog or imported dog;
3. supporting evidence is uploaded;
4. mating and birth are declared;
5. puppies are submitted for registration;
6. a validator requests corrections, rejects, or approves the application;
7. an approved pedigree PDF is generated;
8. validated public information can be searched without exposing private data.

## Included in the MVP

- portal users and canine owners;
- breeds, dogs, identifiers, ownership, and ancestry;
- imported dogs;
- application workflows and evidence;
- mating, birth, litters, and puppy registration;
- validation and correction requests;
- death declaration;
- manual or simulated payment;
- pedigree PDF generation;
- privacy-aware public search;
- audit history;
- fictional demonstration data.

## Not included in the MVP

- real Mobile Money integration;
- migration of an existing production database;
- production deployment for a specific organization;
- advanced accounting;
- a native mobile application;
- partner APIs;
- SMS integration;
- electronic signatures;
- unauthorized third-party branding or content.

## Data and branding policy

Only fictional demonstration data may be committed. Real personal records, animal documents, pedigrees, photographs, payment information, and production exports are prohibited.

The name, logo, content, and production data of ACYM or any other organization may be used only after explicit authorization.
